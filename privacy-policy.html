Privacy Policy

YouTube Comment Overlay Chrome Extension  ·  Last updated: 2026-11-02

1. Overview

YouTube Comment Overlay ("the Extension") displays YouTube video comments as an overlay on the video player. This policy explains what data the Extension accesses and how it is handled.

2. Data Sent to Our Server

To fetch comments, the Extension sends the ID of the YouTube video you open to our backend server (youtube-comment-overlay-server-production.up.railway.app), unless the comments for that video are already saved in your browser. Like any web request, this request also exposes your IP address to the server.

  • Video IDs are used to look up comments. Our server does not link them to you or keep a record of which videos you watched.
  • IP addresses are held in memory for about one minute to limit excessive requests. Our server does not write them to a log or database.
  • Our server runs on Railway, which may process technical request data, such as IP addresses, to operate its hosting service.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

The Extension does not:

  • Ask for or collect your name, email address, or Google or YouTube account information.
  • Build a history or profile of the videos you watch.
  • Sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • Use cookies or any form of cross-site tracking.

4. YouTube API Services

The Extension uses YouTube API Services to obtain public comment data for the video you open. Our server, not your browser, makes these requests to Google, using a key that belongs to the service. You do not need to provide an API key. By using the Extension, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Temporary Storage of Comments

Public comment data, including comment text, commenter display names, and like and reply counts, is cached so that the same video does not need to be requested repeatedly:

  • On our server: by video ID, for up to 24 hours. This cache is shared by all users and contains no information about who requested a video.
  • In your browser: in chrome.storage.local, for about 2 hours. Expired entries are removed as new comments are saved, and all of them are removed when you uninstall the Extension.

6. Your Settings

Your display mode, position, opacity, language, and on/off preference are saved with chrome.storage.sync. If Chrome sync is on, Google syncs these settings across your browsers. They are not sent to our server.

7. Permissions

  • storage — saves your settings and temporarily caches comments.
  • activeTab — lets the Extension act on the active tab when you use it.
  • https://www.youtube.com/* — required to detect the current video and show the overlay.
  • https://youtube-comment-overlay-server-production.up.railway.app/* — required to fetch comments from our server.

8. Children's Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
